Meet Nolan

Hi, I'm Nolan Wayne, a professional dancer and instructor based in Austin. I danced in Dancing with the Stars Austin, but what I love most isn't the spotlight, it's watching someone who swore they "can't dance" light up the first time it finally clicks.

I specialize in beginners. If you've never taken a lesson in your life, you're exactly who I built this for. I break every move down into simple, manageable steps and keep things low-pressure and genuinely fun, whether we're working on your wedding first dance or your very first Ballroom, Country, or Swing lesson.

Everyone has their own reason to dance, and I'm proud to teach all of my students with patience and zero judgment, regardless of gender or orientation. Let's find yours.
