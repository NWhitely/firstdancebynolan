Meet Nolan

Hi, I'm Nolan Wayne, a professional dancer and instructor in Austin. I danced in Dancing with the Stars Austin and compete at the top levels of country-western dance (most recently a pair of 1st-place finishes in Advanced Two-Step at the AskMeToDance comp at Wild West), but the students I love teaching most are the ones who walk in convinced they have no rhythm at all.

I specialize in beginners. If you've never taken a lesson, never had a partner, or just always wanted to learn, you're in exactly the right place. I break everything down into simple steps and keep it low-pressure and genuinely fun, so the only thing you have to bring is a willingness to try.

I teach all of my students with patience and zero judgment, regardless of gender or orientation. Come as you are, and let's get you moving.

Styles I Teach

✦

Pick a style that excites you, or tell me what you'd like to learn and we'll find the right fit. Most students start with zero experience.

Ballroom

Beginner & Social Friendly

Waltz, Foxtrot, Rumba, Cha Cha, and more. Elegant, classic, and a wonderful place to start if you've never danced. We'll build your fundamentals one step at a time.

Country

Beginner to Advanced

Two-step, country waltz, and Texas dance-hall favorites. Great for first-timers, and since I competed at the top levels of country-western, I can also take social and experienced dancers to the next level.

Swing

Beginner & Social Friendly

Bouncy, playful, and a blast at any party. East Coast Swing and nightclub favorites that get you comfortable leading or following on a social floor.
